OUR STORY

Rooted in Jairampur. Ready for the future.

The school began its journey in 1953 as a foundational educational institution in the remote Jairampur region. Over the decades, it grew into a Senior Secondary / Higher Secondary School affiliated with the CBSE board.

In January 2018, a newly constructed school building was inaugurated by Arunachal Pradesh Chief Minister Pema Khandu, strengthening local learning facilities.
